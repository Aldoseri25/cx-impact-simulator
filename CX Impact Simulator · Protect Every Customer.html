How to use this demo

An AI support agent wants to email 500 customers about a planned service disruption. It may not queue or send anything until the send has been simulated.

  1. Draft the send. The agent pulls the 500-row segment as it is.
  2. Read the simulation. Nothing is queued yet. Red problems block approval. Switch fixes on one by one, or press “Apply simulator’s fix”.
  3. Approve & execute when every check is green. The list is rechecked right before sending, then sent in batches.
  4. Stop & roll back any time while sending: queued messages are cancelled, and sent ones get a recorded correction.

To see the safety net work, tick the failure test before approving. Approval is not a confirm dialog: it unlocks only when computed checks pass. Architecture and thesis are at the bottom.
